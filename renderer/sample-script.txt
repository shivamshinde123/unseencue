# Welcome to the Channel
Hey everyone, and welcome back! Today we're building something {#ffd166}really useful{/}: a teleprompter that sits right under your webcam, so you can read your script while you {white|#6b62f2}look straight at your audience{/}.

## Part 1 · Why This Matters
When you read from a second monitor, your eyes drift. Viewers notice it instantly, even if they can't say why. Keeping the words close to the lens makes you look **confident**, *relaxed*, and prepared.

### Quick Facts
- Eye contact builds trust within the first **seven seconds**.
- Most people read about {#4cc9f0}150 words per minute{/} out loud.
- A short pause feels much longer to you than it does to the viewer.

> {black|#ffd166}CUE:{/} Smile here, then lean in slightly toward the camera.

## Part 2 · Writing a Script That Sounds Natural
Write the way you talk. Short sentences. Simple words. If a line feels awkward when you say it out loud, rewrite it, because your audience will hear the awkwardness too.

### Three Rules I Always Follow
1. Start with the **payoff**: tell people what they'll get.
2. Use one idea per paragraph, so every scroll feels like a fresh beat.
3. End each section with a {|#2a2a2a}bridge sentence{/} that pulls viewers into the next one.

> {white|#ff4d6d}B-ROLL:{/} Show the editor screen recording while reading this section.

Here's a tip most creators miss: mark your *emphasis words* in colour. When you see {red}red{/}, you punch the word. When you see {#90be6d}green{/}, you slow down and let it land.

## Part 3 · Recording Day

### Before You Hit Record
- Check your lighting and framing.
- Drink some water, because your voice will thank you.
- Run the {#c2c2c2}hidden-from-recording check{/} in Settings.

### Shot List
| Time | Shot | Note |
|------|------|------|
| 0:00 | Face cam | Smile, then intro |
| 0:45 | Screen recording | Show the settings window |
| 2:10 | Face cam | Wrap up and subscribe ask |

### During the Take
Speak to **one person**, not a crowd. Picture a friend sitting just behind the lens. If you stumble, don't stop: pause, breathe, and pick up from the start of the sentence. You can cut it later.

> {black|#90be6d}PAUSE:{/} Two-second beat before the next line.
