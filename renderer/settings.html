Hidden from recordings & screen shares

The teleprompter and this window are left out of screen recordings and screen shares on Windows 10 (2004 or later) and Windows 11: OBS, Loom, Xbox Game Bar, Snipping Tool, PrintScreen, and screen sharing in Zoom, Meet and Teams. Your webcam video is separate and never shows the screen.
